# Plannly AI Foundry

> Big goals. Small steps. Real progress.

A personal productivity and execution assistant that turns goals and intentions into small, actionable daily steps — reducing the mental burden of planning, remembering, organizing, communicating, and following through.

Designed for anyone who struggles with organization and follow-through, with particular consideration for ADHD and executive-function challenges.

## Core Philosophy

> Big goals become achievable when they are broken into small actions that are visible, manageable, and completed consistently.

Every small action has context: why am I doing this, what does it contribute to, what comes next.

## Core Loop

Goal → Breakdown → Action → Reminder → Completion → Progress → Adaptation

## MVP Focus (per PRD)

1. **Goal-to-Action System** — creation, clarification, breakdown (milestone/monthly/weekly/daily/tiny), daily focus, progress
2. **Personal Assistant** — natural-language planning, re-planning, recovery
3. **Reminders & Scheduling**
4. **Personalized Communication** — email drafting, templates
5. **Workflow Automation**

See `Docx/Plannly-Product Requirements Document (PRD).md` for full spec.
